# Personal Website Structure

## 1. Hero (top of home page)
- Name, title ("Senior Full-Stack Engineer · React/Next.js"), one-line value prop
- Clear CTA: "View my work" / "Get in touch"
- Optional: note on availability (remote, fractional/4-day open)

## 2. About / Bio
- Short narrative: your experience, what you're great at, what kind of problems you like solving
- Keep it human, not a resume dump

## 3. Selected Work / Case Studies
*(Core section — most important on the page)*
- 3–5 projects, each with: problem → your role → tech stack → outcome/impact (metrics if you have them)
- Prioritize depth over quantity — one great case study beats five shallow ones

## 4. Skills / Tech Stack
- Grouped simply (Frontend / Backend / Tools)
- Don't over-design this — it's a scan-and-move-on section

## 5. Experience Timeline (optional)
- Brief, especially if your case studies already tell the story
- Can fold into About if it feels redundant

## 6. Contact / CTA
- Simple form or just email + LinkedIn + calendar link (Calendly)
- Repeat availability/positioning here (e.g. open to fractional/remote roles)

---

## Optional Additions
- **Blog / Writing** — demonstrates thought leadership, good for credibility and SEO
- **Resume/CV download link**

---

## Format Recommendation
Single scrollable page (Hero → About → Work → Skills → Contact) rather than multiple separate pages — lower friction, easier to maintain, most visitors won't click through multiple pages anyway.
